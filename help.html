💌 How to use CardPress

CardPress makes photo cards you print at home – Christmas and holiday cards, New Year and Tết cards, birthdays, thank-you notes, weddings, new babies and more. Everything stays on this PC.

1 · Design

Pick the occasion, the card type (flat 7 × 5 or 5 × 7 cards, 2 per sheet; Pro adds square cards, postcards and folded cards), the paper (Letter or A4), a font, a layout and a design.

2 · Words

Choose a greeting or write your own, then the message and how you sign it. Untick “Show the year” if you don't want a year on the card.

3 · Photos

Click a photo frame on the card (or ＋ Add or replace photo). Drag the photo to move it and use the zoom slider or your mouse wheel to zoom. Photos are made smaller to save space, but stay sharp enough to print.

4 · Inside & back

Folded cards (Pro) have an inside greeting and message. Flat cards can have a back side (Pro) with family news and a small photo. Tick Personal names (Pro) to start each card with “Dear …” for everyone ticked “Send” on your card list.

5 · Card list and labels

Keep the people you send cards to, with their address. Tick ✉ to send, and 📬 when you got a card from them. With Pro, paste or import a list (CSV) and print address labels and return-address labels on Avery 5160 / 8160, 5163 or L7160 sheets. Export CSV is always free.

6 · Print

Click 🖨 Print (or Ctrl+P). Choose how many cards. Flat cards print 2 per sheet – cut along the dotted lines. For a back side or a folded card, print the first side, put the sheets back into the printer turned over, then print the second side.

Print one test sheet on plain paper first to see which way your printer turns the paper. In the print window, choose the right paper size and set margins to “None” or “Default”; keep the scale at 100% (“Actual size”). Card stock of 65–110 lb (176–300 g/m²) looks best.

Photo shop prints (Pro)

☰ More → Save for a photo shop saves a 5 × 7 JPEG at 300 dpi you can order as photo prints. Save as a picture saves a PNG to share by message or email.
